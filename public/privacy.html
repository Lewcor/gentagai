Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** [DATE]

This Privacy Policy explains how GENTAGAI ("we," "us," "our"), operated by [LEGAL ENTITY NAME], collects, uses, and protects your information when you use gentagai.com and related applications (the "Service").

1. Information We Collect

Account information. When you sign up, we collect your email address (via magic-link authentication through Supabase) and your subscription plan and billing status.

Brand and content data. Information you provide to use the Service, including: brand names, niches, target audiences, tone preferences, campaign goals, SEO keywords, product details, and any images or videos you upload for AI analysis or your Brand Vault.

Generated content. The marketing copy, image prompts, video scripts, and other content GENTAGAI generates for you, which we store so you can access your generation history.

Payment information. Subscription payments are processed by Stripe. We do not store your full credit card number — Stripe handles that directly under its own privacy policy. We retain records of your subscription status, plan, and billing history.

Third-party API keys (optional). If you choose to connect your own Google Gemini or OpenAI API key, we store that key so the Service can use it on your behalf. You can remove it at any time from your account settings.

Social media connections (optional). If you connect a social media account via our publishing integration (Postiz), we store the resulting access tokens needed to publish content on your behalf, along with basic account information from the connected platform (e.g., account name, profile picture).

Usage data. We track generation counts, feature usage, and similar operational data to enforce plan limits and improve the Service.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information above to:

  • Operate and provide the Service, including generating AI marketing content tailored to your brand
  • Process payments and manage your subscription
  • Enable optional features you choose to use (social publishing, custom AI providers, asset storage)
  • Maintain and improve the Service's reliability and performance
  • Communicate with you about your account, billing, or material changes to the Service
  • Comply with legal obligations

We do not sell your personal information.

3. How Your Information Is Shared

We share information only as necessary to operate the Service, with the following categories of service providers:

ProviderPurpose
**Supabase**Authentication, database storage, and file storage for your account, brand data, and uploads
**Stripe**Payment processing and subscription billing
**Anthropic (Claude)**Generating your marketing content by processing the brand/product information and images you submit
**Google (Gemini) / OpenAI (ChatGPT)**Only if you connect your own API key for these providers, to generate content using that provider instead

Each of these providers processes data under their own privacy policies and terms. We choose providers that maintain reasonable security practices, but we don't control their infrastructure directly.

We may also disclose information if required by law, to protect our rights, or in connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets (with notice to you where required).

Your data is stored using Supabase's managed database and storage infrastructure. We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information, including encrypted connections (HTTPS) and access controls. No system is perfectly secure, and we can't guarantee absolute security of your data.
